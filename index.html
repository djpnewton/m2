Monetary mechanics · modelled on the Federal Reserve

How interest rates and bond purchases become M2

Money is created when banks lend, and when a central bank buys assets from someone other than a bank. M2 is the money you can spend without notice — notes and coins plus on-call bank deposits. This page is a working model of both: move the levers and watch the money stock, the base the central bank pays with, and the multiplier that connects them.

Assumptions worth knowing

A teaching model, not a forecast. All figures are in billions of local currency unless stated. The model solves no optimisation problem and has no expectations beyond a simple backward-looking anchor — it exists to make the accounting visible.
